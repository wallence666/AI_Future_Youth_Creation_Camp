## Notes (non-blocking)
- `detect.mjs`'s `em-dash-overuse` hit (7 instances) resolves entirely to non-shipping text: HTML/CSS developer comments, the wireframe's own meta-explanatory intro/footnote copy (explicitly marked "非最終文案" — not final copy), and bracketed wireframe placeholders (`[地圖 Leaflet — 熱力層 active]`, `[折線圖 — ...]`). Zero em-dashes appear in actual in-app strings (spot name, description, button labels, info rows). Register-justified — Note only, not a content-design defect.
- `.band-badge` in the drawer header hardcodes `background: var(--band-fill-high)` with no `.low`/`.mid` modifier classes, unlike `.dot`/`.sw` which both have all three variants. Not a visible defect in this render (the only state shown is 擁擠/high, and it renders correctly), but worth resolving before other band states are exercised on this component.
- Positive: the map markers' three-way grey-value differentiation (hollow/mid-grey/solid-dark, all sharing a consistent dark 2px ring) is clear and well-separated at marker scale, satisfying the colorblind-safe redundant-encoding intent the page states for itself — a deliberate decision a generic template would likely skip in favor of hue alone.
- Distinctiveness (ai-tells CHECKER mode): not applied as a pass/fail axis here. DW-MOCK.2 explicitly instructs judging structure/density/hierarchy rather than color/visual identity at this pre-DESIGN.md wireframe stage, and the surface's information architecture (shape-redundant crowd bands, methodology transparency, forecast strip, best-time callout) reflects real domain-specific decisions rather than generic boilerplate — so it is not treated as a genericness failure. The `nested-cards` finding above is the concrete, catchable proxy for future genericness risk and is captured there instead.
